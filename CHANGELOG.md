# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- Renamed the application to PK Business ERP Suite.
- Preserved Version 1 login behavior, styling, LocalStorage operation and core navigation concepts.
- Added automatic Version 1 LocalStorage migration for partners, investments, feed purchases, production, feed sales, expenses, fuel purchases, machine readings, staff, settings and audit logs.
- Expanded the dashboard with business-unit cards, daily KPIs, cash/credit summaries, outstanding balances, inventory value, low stock, recent activities, quick actions, notifications and monthly charts.
- Expanded Feed Unit with partner, investment, supplier, purchase, raw material, formula, production, cost, finished goods, bag inventory, sales, customer, expense, staff, salary, report and settings modules.
- Added two separate Chakki businesses: customer-owned wheat grinding service and company flour bag production/sales.
- Expanded Petrol Pump with fuel purchase, tank stock, Machine 1/2 readings, automatic fuel-sold calculation, customer credit, temporary credit, cash, expenses, salary, reports and closings.
- Added Super Store, Gas Management and consolidated Cash Book modules.
- Added daily/monthly/yearly reports, ledgers and print previews.
- Added notifications, backup, restore, users, roles, permissions and activity logs.
